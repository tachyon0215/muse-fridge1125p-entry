# muse-fridge1125p-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [One Line, One Laugh: Roast Your Fridge](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suzy-one-line-one-laugh-roast-your-fridge-f1063521).

## The entry

> My fridge keeps a light on all night for food it will throw out on Friday — it's not storage, it's a tiny, cold waiting room for regrets.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: one specific detail (the all-night light for food thrown out on Friday) and one image a stranger gets instantly; the other candidates split the target between two appliances or stopped at setup.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 c846ec559d0e...).
